Parking & business

Most parking stays, and the state pays

A road diet changes the travel lanes, not the curb. Most parking on Boston Post Road stays where it is, and the project costs local taxpayers nothing.

Parking

Most existing parking remains. Some spaces would be removed or moved where the room is needed for safety:

  • Longer bus stops, so buses can pull fully to the curb instead of stopping in a travel lane.
  • Clear sight lines at intersections, so drivers turning out of side streets can see people crossing and cars coming.
  • Curb extensions, which shorten the distance people have to walk across the road.

NYSDOT is still refining the parking layout. The latest version is in the project documents.
