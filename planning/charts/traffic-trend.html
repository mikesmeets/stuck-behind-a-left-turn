Boston Post Road · daily traffic

Post Road traffic hasn't grown in 45 years, and it fell after 2019

Mamaroneck is down a quarter since 2019, and Larchmont is down a quarter since 2014. Three of the four counted segments are at their lowest in the record.

    Average annual daily traffic (AADT), both directions. Each dot is a year NYSDOT counted that segment. Segments are counted every few years, not every year, so the lines connect counts and don't show what happened in between.

    Source: NYSDOT, Traffic Data Viewer.
