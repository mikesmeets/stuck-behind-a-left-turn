Safety

Safer for everyone on the road, starting with drivers

Safety is the reason for this project. Most of the crashes a road diet prevents are between cars: rear-ends, sideswipes and left turns. The people walking and biking to our schools benefit too.

Why this road crashes

Boston Post Road squeezes four travel lanes into a width that barely fits them. Each lane is about 9.5 feet wide, where the standard is 11. That leaves little room for error.

Then add the turns. Driveways, side streets and parking spaces line the corridor, and every driver waiting to turn left stops in a travel lane. The drivers behind brake hard or swerve into the next lane, and on 9.5-foot lanes both go wrong often.
