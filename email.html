Email your officials

A short letter to the people deciding what happens to Boston Post Road. Add a sentence of your own at the top: one personal line counts for far more than a form letter.

Send it

Pick whichever works on your device. If a button does nothing, your browser has no mail app attached to it: use Copy the letter and paste it into a new email.

Who it goes to

The Town of Mamaroneck board, both village boards, State Senator Shelley Mayer, Assembly Member Steven Otis, County Legislator Anant Nambiar and NYSDOT’s design project manager for the corridor, Christopher Hann. Rep. George Latimer has no public email address; his office takes messages through his website.
