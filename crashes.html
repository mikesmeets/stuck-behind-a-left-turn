What happens on Boston Post Road now

The numbers behind what people who use the Post Road already know: crashes are common, they cluster around the schools, and some are fatal.

NYSDOT puts serious injuries in the same category as deaths when it looks for dangerous places.

Those are people on foot and on bikes. Counting everyone on the road, five people have been killed in three crashes on one 1.5-mile stretch of the Post Road since 2017.

On Boston Post Road, the crashes cluster around the schools, especially between the Hommocks and Mamaroneck High School.

The map also shows separate clusters around the Larchmont and Mamaroneck train stations and along Mamaroneck Avenue. Those matter too, but they're a different problem from the crashes near the schools.

These numbers are almost certainly low. When a child on a bike is knocked down and isn't hurt, they usually get up and keep going, and no report is filed.

Don't wait for the fatal crash. The evidence is already here.

In 2024, a mother and her son were struck and killed at the traffic signal at Mamaroneck Avenue and New Street. That's not on Boston Post Road, but the lesson carries over. The signal had no pedestrian-only phase, no moment when every car stops so people can cross. The danger at that corner was widely known. The changes came after they died.

On Boston Post Road, the danger is known now, the fix is proven, and the state has the money set aside. There's no reason to wait.
